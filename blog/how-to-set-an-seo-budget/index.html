How to Set an SEO Budget (Without Guessing)

How to set an SEO marketing budget: work backward from customer value, decide what you can do yourself, and split the budget across the work that matters.

Every dollar gets a job.

By Zach Wennstedt, founder of Eye To Ad Media · September 29, 2026

To set an SEO budget, work backward from what a new customer is worth and how many extra customers you need, decide which tasks you can do yourself, then fund the remaining expert work, split across technical fixes, local optimization, content, links, AI search readiness and conversion. Fix conversion before buying more traffic.

Most businesses set an SEO budget the way people pick a restaurant tip: a number that feels about right. Here is a better way.

Step 1: Know what a customer is worth

Average first job value, or better, lifetime value. A plumber's customer might be worth a few hundred dollars on the first visit and much more over years.

Step 2: Decide how many extra customers would change things

If three extra customers a month would cover the cost, SEO has a clear bar to clear. If you need a hundred, the budget and timeline change completely.

Step 3: Split DIY and expert work

Owners are often great at reviews, photos and profile updates. Experts earn their fee on technical fixes, content strategy, links and conversion. Use the free SEO tools cost calculator to price your own time honestly.

Step 4: Split the money

A weak website should get more technical and conversion money first. A strong site can lean into content and links. The free SEO budget planner builds a starting split for you.

Step 5: Commit to a review date

Give a real plan several months, then judge it on calls and leads, not rankings alone.

FAQ

Straight answers

What percentage of revenue should go to SEO?

There is no universal percentage. It is better to work backward from customer value and the number of extra customers you need, then fund the work that can produce them.

Should I spend on SEO or ads first?

Ads buy immediate traffic and data. SEO builds a compounding channel. Many small businesses run both, and fix conversion before scaling either.
