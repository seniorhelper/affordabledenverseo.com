Local SEO packages

Local SEO packages for businesses that live on nearby customers.

When someone searches "near me," Google shows three businesses on a map. Those three get most of the calls. A local SEO package is how you become one of them without a big-agency budget.

Local SEO packages are monthly plans focused on ranking a business in Google Maps and local search results. They typically include Google Business Profile optimization, review generation, consistent citations and listings, locally targeted pages, LocalBusiness schema and reporting on calls, direction requests and form leads.

Local SEO is usually the best value per dollar in all of search marketing. You are not competing with the whole internet. You are competing with a handful of businesses within driving distance, and most of them have half-finished profiles.

That is the opening. Here is what a local package should do with it.

What is inside a local SEO package

Google Business Profile

Primary and secondary categories, services, photos, hours, products and posts. The single highest-return local task.

Reviews system

A simple, honest routine for asking every happy customer, plus responses to every review.

Citations and listings

Your name, address and phone identical across directories. Inconsistency confuses Google and AI assistants.

Local pages

Service and area pages that say something real about each place you serve, not city-name swaps.

LocalBusiness schema

Structured data that tells machines exactly who you are, where and when.

Call-focused reporting

Calls, direction requests and forms from Maps and search, month by month.

Local SEO package pricing

Our local work starts with the Local Starter plan at $597 a month, which covers Google Business Profile, citations, on-page work and conversion review. Businesses in tougher markets or with several locations usually fit Denver Growth from $1,197. See them side by side on the SEO packages page.

Listings matter more in the AI era

AI assistants like ChatGPT and Google's AI features cross-check your business details across the web before recommending you. Consistent listings on trusted directories, such as USA Business Search, make that check easy to pass.

FAQ

Straight answers

What do local SEO packages include?

Typically Google Business Profile optimization, review generation, citation and listing cleanup, local service or area pages, LocalBusiness schema and reporting on calls and direction requests.

How much do local SEO packages cost?

Local SEO packages for small businesses commonly start in the hundreds of dollars a month. Ours start at $597 a month with the Local Starter plan.

How long does local SEO take to work?

Profile and listing fixes can show movement within weeks to a few months. Competitive markets and new businesses take longer, and results compound with consistent reviews and content.

Do I need a website for local SEO?

You can rank in Maps with a strong Google Business Profile alone, but a fast website with local pages helps you rank higher, win AI recommendations and convert visitors.
