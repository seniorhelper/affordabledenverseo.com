Link building packages

Links you earn are worth more than links you buy.

Search "cheap backlinks" and you will find thousands of offers. Most of them sell exactly what Google's spam policies describe as link spam. Here is how we build authority instead.

Link building packages are services that help a website gain links from other websites, a key signal of authority in search. Safe link building earns links through press releases, news coverage, genuine directory listings, partnerships and content worth citing. Buying links or using link networks violates Google's spam policies.

Google's spam policies are clear: buying or selling links to manipulate rankings, excessive link exchanges and private blog networks are all link spam. The sites selling "100 backlinks for $50" are counting on you not reading that page.

How we earn links instead

Press releases with substance

A real announcement published and distributed through a press release wire can earn coverage and citations.

News and industry coverage

Stories, data or expert commentary that journalists and industry sites want to reference.

Real directory listings

Genuine, relevant directories where customers actually look, with consistent details.

Partners and associations

Suppliers, chambers, associations and local sponsorships that already know you.

Content worth citing

Guides, tools and local data that other sites link to on their own.

Unlinked mentions

Finding places that already mention you and asking for the link.

Network resources we use

Our team runs publishing and distribution channels used in client campaigns: Press Release For Business for publishing releases, Distribute Press Releases for wider distribution, Action Global News for business and technology stories, and the USA Business Search directory for consistent business listings. We use them only where they fit the story and the business.

FAQ

Straight answers

Are cheap link building packages safe?

Usually not. Cheap packages typically rely on bought links, link networks or spammy directories, which Google's spam policies classify as link spam.

How many backlinks do I need?

There is no magic number. A few relevant, trusted links often matter more than hundreds of weak ones. Quality and relevance beat volume.

Do press releases help SEO?

A press release with a real story can earn coverage, citations and brand mentions. Links inside wire copy are typically treated as nofollow or sponsored, so the value comes from the coverage and awareness it creates.

What is a private blog network?

A group of websites built to link to other sites for ranking purposes. It is a link scheme under Google's spam policies.
