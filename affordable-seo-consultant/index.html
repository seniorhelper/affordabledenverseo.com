That is it?

An affordable SEO consultant provides expert search strategy, audits and advice without a long-term retainer. Consulting suits businesses that can do some of the work themselves but need a professional to set priorities, review a site or proposal, fix a specific problem, or coach an in-house person.

Here is the honest trade-off. A consultant is cheaper per month than a full agency plan because you do more of the work. If you have the hours, that is a great deal. If you do not, the plan will sit in a folder and you will have paid for good intentions.

Specific problem

Traffic dropped, a migration went wrong, or your Google profile got suspended.

DIY coaching

You or a team member will do the work and want an expert checking it.

FAQ

Straight answers

What does an SEO consultant do?

An SEO consultant reviews your site and market, sets priorities, advises on strategy, solves specific problems and coaches the people doing the work, without necessarily doing all of the work themselves.

Is an SEO consultant cheaper than an agency?

Usually per month, because you or your team do more of the hands-on work. It is only cheaper overall if the work actually gets done.

Can a consultant review my current SEO agency?

Yes. A second opinion on an agency's reports and deliverables is one of the most common and valuable consulting requests.

How do I find an affordable SEO expert?

Look for someone who explains things plainly, asks about your business goals first, reports on outcomes, and can show real past work. Avoid anyone who guarantees rankings.
