FAQ

Straight answers

What is included in a website and SEO package?

Keyword and page planning, design and development, fast code, structured data, on-page optimization, migration of existing rankings, and conversion paths such as click-to-call and short forms.

Is it cheaper to do web design and SEO together?

Usually. Building search requirements into the site from the start avoids paying to rebuild or fix pages after launch.

Will a new website hurt my rankings?

It can if old URLs are changed without redirects or ranking content is removed. A proper migration maps every indexed URL first.

Can I pay for a website monthly?

Yes. Qualifying businesses can get a custom site from $99 a month on a subscribe-to-own plan, with a buyout option at 24 to 36 months.
