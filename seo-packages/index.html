How to compare SEO packages from different agencies

Line up the work, not the labels. For each package ask: how many pages get optimized, how much new content gets published, how links are earned, what technical work is included, and what the report measures. If a package lists "100 backlinks" or "500 directory submissions," run it through our SEO quote checker first.

FAQ

Straight answers

How much do SEO packages cost?

Legitimate SEO packages for small and mid-sized businesses commonly start in the hundreds to low thousands of dollars a month, depending on competition and scope. Ours start at $597, $1,197 and $2,497 a month and are tailored after a free call.

What should an SEO package include?

Technical fixes, on-page optimization, content, local work such as Google Business Profile, link earning, structured data, conversion improvements and reporting on calls and leads. The mix depends on your market and goals.

Are cheaper SEO packages worth it?

Sometimes, if the scope is honest and focused. Very cheap packages often rely on automated reports, bulk directory submissions or bought links, which can waste money or cause penalties.

Can I change packages later?

Yes. Most businesses start with a focused scope and add work as results come in or as they expand into new markets.

Do your packages include AI search optimization?

Yes. Every package includes AI search readiness, and higher tiers add dedicated AIO and GEO content structuring and monitoring.
